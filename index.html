Mouse, touch & gamepad controls

Launch, then click the view to capture the mouse. Move the mouse to look; stop moving to stop turning. Mouse look levels roll and limits pitch. WASD thrusts; Space / Ctrl rises / descends. Arrow keys and Q / E retain flight turning and roll. Escape releases the mouse and pauses; resume, then click to capture again. Touch or unavailable mouse capture: drag to look. On a standard gamepad: left stick strafes / thrusts; right stick turns; shoulder buttons roll; triggers descend / rise; A brakes; Menu pauses. Release all gamepad controls after launch or resume. Losing focus pauses the experiment; resume deliberately.
