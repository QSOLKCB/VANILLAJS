Controls & on-screen movement

Launch, then click the view to capture the mouse. WASD moves, Space jumps, Shift sprints, C crouches, arrows aim, F or captured left click fires, R reloads. Escape releases the cursor and pauses. Resume, then click to capture again. Touch or denied capture: drag to look. Release and press controls again after pause or focus loss. Release C to stand; a low ceiling keeps you safely crouched. Static courses support stairs; the platform courses support translating lifts, riding and jump-off momentum. Slopes and rotating platforms remain future work.
