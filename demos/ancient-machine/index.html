NOTES FROM THE WORKBENCH

Old-world form.
Finite-state mathematics.

This imagined instrument gives the OMI rule a mechanical face. The brass, cams and drums illustrate discrete integer operations; this is not a reconstruction of a historical device or a continuous analog solver.

Δ(x) = rotl(x, 1) ⊕ rotl(x, 3)
⊕ rotr(x, 2) ⊕ 0x5A3C

All 65,536 words return after eight revolutions. Some return sooner. The four drums use VANILLAJS’s nibble encoding of FS, GS, RS and US.
