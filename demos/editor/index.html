    Play mode

    Component-driven fixed 60 Hz preview with continuous AABB collision, editable speed and jump impulse, and trigger-enter events. WASD moves; Space jumps. Stop restores the authored scene.

    Engine boundaries

    Box mesh editor with X/Y/Z move and scale, Y rotation, snapping and authored components. Dynamic-dynamic and rotated collisions and scripting are not implemented. Local meshes, materials and prefabs use Asset Foundry; playable HTML needs the packaged modules beside it.
