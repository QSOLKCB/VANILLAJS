    Field guide · controls, replay and attribution

    Move: WASD; Shift sprints, Space jumps, C crouches. Click the view to capture the mouse; Escape releases it and pauses. Drag to look on touch or use the arrow keys. Release held movement controls after pausing or closing a console.

    Operate: approach the next console and press E. Use the crank or T for a Δ turn; 1–3 pull levers, U undoes, R resets the current seal. Tab between controls; Enter or Space activates a focused button. Engage the seal when its word matches. The motor completes one revolution at a time. Closing a console stops its motor; pause holds its exact position. A released seal stays released.
