Your mapping, your music

This is a creative instrument inspired by E8_MUSIC and SONIFICATION. The pitches, tempo and timbre are authored choices. It uses VANILLAJS's existing 240-root generator and integer audio renderer; it does not reproduce ETQ-101/303, import donor audio or claim a natural E8 sound.

Exports are 48 kHz mono PCM16, at most 8 seconds. Playback stops when this page loses focus; click Listen to restart. Device playback may resample the exported audio.

3. Put it in a game

Use the WAV as a short menu, victory or checkpoint cue. To render a game score in code, pass its frames and events to createSound2D. Keep playback behind a player action and stop it on pause.
