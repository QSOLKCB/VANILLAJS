Select a model and launch. No game state advances until you start.

Controls

W/S throttle or drive; A/D strafe (space) or steer (car); arrows pitch/yaw; Q/E roll. F fires the original aligned gun-ray test; M launches a guided arcade missile when physically locked. Missiles work in aircraft and space modes, not cars. Click the 3D view for optional mouse head-look: turning your head moves the sight relative to the camera, never the guns.

Gunsights are approximate reflector-style art. The real Spitfire used a reflector gunsight rather than a modern flight HUD. The propeller model is an original arcade approximation, not historical aircraft flight certification. All data and textures are generated locally.
