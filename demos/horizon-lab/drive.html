This bounded model approximates tyres, suspension and mass distribution. The flat handling ground isolates steering and braking; the ramp exposes airtime and landing; Aster Coast adds elevation, bank and separate road/terrain surfaces. Orange blocks are swept chassis barriers. Near-vertical terrain faces (normal Y below 0.15) are not support surfaces. Trees, buildings, bridge masonry and tunnel shells are visual scenery.

No race rules or opponent are included. Vehicle physics runs at 120 Hz; camera, wheel drawing and materials do not change forces. Pausing stops simulation and continuous drawing.
