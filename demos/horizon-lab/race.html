Both cars use fixed-step vehicle dynamics: four wheel contacts, engine torque, gears, grip and suspension. The rival steers and brakes through the same world. Camera motion and sound never change those forces.

This is a bounded vehicle approximation. The road and terrain support the wheels. Bridge guardrails constrain the car near the deck edges. When the two cars make contact at the same height, they push against each other. Trees, checkpoint arches and tunnel shells are decorative. Recovery returns the car to the last completed gate and adds a visible time penalty. Cockpit is a dashboard-style driving view, not a detailed interior model.
