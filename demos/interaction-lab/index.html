THE MODEL HAS BOUNDARIES

A playable rule model.

The hydrogen–oxygen example shows reactant availability, an ignition condition and water formation. Neon illustrates resistance under ordinary conditions. These are symbolic probes and reusable specimens: chamber stock is not depleted, and energy, amounts, reaction rates and temperature are not calculated.

All controls & on-screen flight deck

Arrow keys or drag the view to turn. F fires. Escape pauses or resumes. In Target practice: WASD thrusts, Space / Ctrl rises / descends, Q / E rolls, X brakes. Reaction Bench stays at its observation station. Focus buttons line up a specimen. Blur and hidden tabs pause the simulation; held controls must be released and pressed again.
