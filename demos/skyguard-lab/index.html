DDG RESOLUTE / WATCH ORDERS

FOUR WAYS TO CLEAR THE SKY.

  1. TWIN / 35 autocannon: fast tracers with travel time. Aim ahead of aircraft; the small diamond estimates an intercept. Fire short bursts and release to cool.
  2. FUSE / 76 flak: shells burst at the aim point after about half a second, or sooner near an aircraft. Lead a cluster to catch it in the blast.
  3. SEASPEAR VLS: keep the reticle within the targeting ring until LOCKED, then hold fire. Each interceptor homes on its acquired aircraft. Lose the lock and the launcher waits.
  4. LANCER railgun: hold fire until fully charged, then release for a piercing beam. Three aligned aircraft can be hit before refits. Early release vents the charge without spending ammunition.

Cadet begins with 8 contacts, with at most four active at once. Each raid grows. Choose one of three refits after clearing a raid; it stays with that battery. The ship restores 8 integrity between raids. Aircraft reaching the ship damage its hull; zero integrity ends the watch.

Controls: pointer / WASD / arrows aim; left mouse / Space fires; R reloads; P / Esc pauses. On touch, drag the sky with one finger and use the separate fire button with another. Standard gamepad: left stick aims, RT / A fires, X reloads, Start pauses. Display changes and lost focus pause combat and cancel held inputs.

Bluewater Watch: a deterministic naval arrangement adapted from Spooky’s Last Light March. FX and music have separate switches; audio failure cannot stop the game.

An original fictional arcade scenario adapted from Spooky v0.1.0. No real weapon training or external game assets. Built with native JavaScript and VANILLAJS.
