WHAT THE EXPERIMENT MEANS

Closure is a relation.

We pack four nibble values into one word, assign them to FS, GS, RS and US, and label each edge with the XOR of its endpoints. XOR around every triangular face returns zero.

The six edges alone do not identify the word: sixteen different words share each edge pattern. Closure also cannot detect every coordinated change.
