    Flight manual / How to play

    Make your first foothold.

    Choose an unsurveyed star next to your territory and issue Survey. Once you know the system, Colonize it to grow your empire. Orders use a limited budget each turn; End turn restores it and gives the rival a move.

    Build an economy.

    Every colony generates ore and science. Upgrade mines to gather ore, build labs for science, and invest in research to strengthen your empire. Accumulate 120 unspent science with at least four colonies to ascend. Build ships in your own colonies, then choose a departure and a destination to dispatch a fleet.

    Hold the frontier.

    Fleet dispatch resolves immediately along a shortest hyperlane route through your own colonies. Enemy ships and colony defenses resist attacks. Watch the journal and protect your capital. The rival develops and expands each turn.

    Own the experiment.

    The sector seed and accepted orders reproduce a run. Export a replay to keep your game; import it to resume. Nothing advances while you are away. This local strategy prototype has no account, server or external assets.
