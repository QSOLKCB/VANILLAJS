PHYSICS DESK

Make the car misbehave.

Native 2D Canvas and original low-poly WebGL. Scenic route forks, a 3.6 km point-to-point 3D road, 60 Hz fixed simulation, fast-pass collision detection, traction loss, impact damage, nitro, airborne flips and respawns. No external assets.

Float64 demo logic is not a proof of cross-browser bit-for-bit replay. Exact Q16.16 circuits remain in the separate Circuit Builder.

Controls / touch / operating notes

W/↑ accelerator · S/↓ reverse / brake · A D/← → steering · Shift boost · X handbrake / drift · Space jump · F aerial barrel roll · Esc pause. Scenic Tour chooses a left or right fork from your lane position at each 1,200 m stage end. Coastal Sprint follows an original 3.6 km winding road. Skyline Stunt has two laps and real gaps. Demolition requires four knockouts. Car Football is first to five goals. Controls cancel on blur, page hide or pause.
